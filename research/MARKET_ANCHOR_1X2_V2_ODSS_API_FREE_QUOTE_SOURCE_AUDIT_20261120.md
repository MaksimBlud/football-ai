**Audit time:** 2026-11-20 18:48:18 UTC  
**Decision:** `HOLD_NO_INTEGRATION`  
**Scope:** public documentation and unauthenticated metadata only. This is not a provider approval, not a model run, and not a change to the frozen V2 quote-source contract.

## Executive result

ODSS API is a more plausible *internal research* quote-source candidate than TheRundown Free: its published Free plan is €0, 500 requests per 30-day period, 30 requests/minute, up to 5,000 records per request, and it explicitly lists internal model/bot/analysis use on every plan. Its normalized feed includes pre-match 1X2 and per-book timestamps.

It is **not admitted to original MARKET_ANCHOR_1X2_V2**. The frozen V2 protocol currently reads existing saved Serie A 1X2 observations from the existing Supabase ledger and explicitly says the workflow never calls an odds provider. Adding ODSS would change the frozen quote-source contract and the market benchmark. The user has prohibited contract changes and Supabase writes. No account/key was created and no authenticated odds request was made.

## Verified public capability and quota

Official pages and OpenAPI describe:

- Free plan: €0, no card, 500 requests per month/30-day quota window, 30 requests/minute.
- One `/odds` request can return up to 5,000 records; the default page size is 500. Stable `after` cursor pagination is documented.
- `sport=calcio`, `market=1x2`, `state=prematch` (default), and league/date filters are available.
- Each record carries event/league/team identity, `commence_time`, market, and bookmaker outcomes `HOME/DRAW/AWAY`; suspended outcomes are separate from missing outcomes.
- The provider documents a deterministic event ID across markets for its own feed. It must **not** be assumed to equal existing Supabase/The Odds API event IDs.
- `/odds/history` is explicitly not active and returns `history_enabled:false`; there is no historical backfill to recover missed prospective quotes.

Official references:

## Timestamp semantics: important for source-before-quote

The OpenAPI contract says:

- `last_update` is the provider engine's last-read time for that bookmaker price. It is **not** a native timestamp generated by the bookmaker.
- `generated_at` identifies the served API snapshot.
- A bookmaker-specific `start_time` can override the event's `commence_time`; the effective kickoff must be checked per book.
- The provider's own documentation warns that a price may be stale and that missing/suspended outcomes are distinct states.

If a separate quote-source protocol were ever authorized, a valid observation would need to preserve the raw response bytes, response `generated_at`, local receipt time, each bookmaker's `last_update`, and effective kickoff (`start_time` if present, otherwise `commence_time`). The feature-source archive must complete strictly before the quote observation time; all three 1X2 legs must be present and unsuspended; and both quote time and effective kickoff must be strictly pre-match. A request/receipt timestamp alone cannot replace the provider's per-book observation time.

## Rights and retention

The provider's public data/licensing page and terms state:

- Free includes internal use: models, bots, and analysis.
- Free does not grant rights to show quotes or derived quote-based displays to third parties. External display requires a separate license.
- Raw data/API redistribution is restricted; value-betting/arbitrage opportunities or signals for third parties require a separate, specifically authorized product/license.
- The reviewed Free-plan materials do not clearly establish a long-term raw-response retention period for a private prospective archive. That point requires written confirmation before retaining raw quote archives beyond transient operational use.
- Historical odds capture is currently disabled.

This audit is not legal advice and does not treat provider marketing statements as a substitute for the accepted terms or a written clarification.

## Re-entry conditions

Keep this candidate on HOLD unless all of the following are satisfied without changing the original V2 contract:

1. The owner explicitly authorizes account creation and a free-key smoke test.
2. The provider confirms private raw-response retention rights in writing for the required prospective archive period.
3. The exact Serie A fixture and bookmaker coverage, full 1X2 completeness, response timestamps, per-book `last_update`, and effective kickoff are demonstrated on a no-cost test.
4. A separately frozen, owner-approved quote-source protocol is available. Until then, ODSS data must not be mixed into the existing Supabase quote ledger or used as an original V2 market benchmark.

**Conclusion:** promising free internal research feed, but not currently admissible to the frozen original MARKET_ANCHOR_1X2_V2 workflow. HOLD is the only scientifically and contractually safe decision.
