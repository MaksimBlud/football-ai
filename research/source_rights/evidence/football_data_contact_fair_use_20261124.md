## Observed policy (paraphrase)

The provider limits its free CSV/Excel data to private personal-betting or academic-research use. Access is intended to be manual or, at most, lightly automated at no more than two download sessions per week. The provider says the data is not intended for commercial or AI data-training projects involving automated bots or scrapers, and says users should assume no permission if they seek permission for those uses and receive no response.
