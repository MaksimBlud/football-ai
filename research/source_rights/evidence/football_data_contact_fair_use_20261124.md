## Observed policy (paraphrase)

The data page describes the free CSV/Excel data as intended for private individuals only and excludes commercial or data-training products using automated bots, scrapers, or AI. The contact page narrows the intended use to personal betting or academic research, allows manual access or at most light automated downloads capped at two sessions per week, and says the data is not intended for commercial or AI data-training projects involving automated bots or scrapers. It also says users should assume no permission if they request permission for those uses and receive no response.

## Project control

This evidence does not grant rights. The original V2 project keeps automated source capture, frozen-model inference, and AI model training denied; provider authorization remains null.
