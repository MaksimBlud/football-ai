## Observed policy (paraphrase)

The page says the free CSV/Excel data are intended for private individuals for personal betting and/or academic research only, accessed manually or through at most light automated downloading (no more than twice per week). It says the data are not intended for commercial or AI data-training projects involving automated bots or scrapers. It further says that if permission is requested for such uses and no response is received, users should assume no data-use permission has been granted.

## Project control

This is a paraphrase of published terms, not a provider-issued licence. The two-downloads-per-week ceiling is not treated as permission. The original V2 project keeps automated source capture, frozen-model inference, AI training, commercial use, and public-product use denied pending applicable provider-issued authorization. This is a conservative internal control, not a legal conclusion about every possible form of personal or academic research.
